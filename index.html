01THE KCC STORY

MORE THAN A SCHOOL

A heritage of
purposeful learning.

Since 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College has been part of the educational story of Kabankalan. In 2026, its mission continues through learning, faith formation, and service.

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is a Catholic educational institution in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental. Its long-standing commitment to Catholic education brings together academic learning, faith formation, and service to the community.

At KCC, education is understood as the formation of the whole person: mind, character, spirit, and a heart ready to serve.
